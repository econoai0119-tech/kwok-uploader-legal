Privacy Policy

Kwok Uploader ("the App") — Last updated: 2026-10-24

Kwok Uploader is a private, single-operator tool used to publish original short-form videos of the animated character "Kwok" to the operator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no third-party users.

1. Who operates the App

Operator: Lee Hyunwoo (individual creator, Republic of Korea). Contact: lhw040924@gmail.com.

2. What data the App accesses

  • Account identity of the operator's own accounts (user ID, display name, avatar) via YouTube Data API, TikTok Login Kit / Content Posting API, and Instagram Graph API — used only to confirm which account a video is published to.
  • Authorization tokens issued by Google, TikTok and Meta — stored locally on the operator's computer in an environment file and never transmitted elsewhere.
  • Video files, titles, captions and publishing settings created by the operator — sent to the respective platform only when the operator publishes.
  • Public performance metrics of the operator's own posts (views, likes, comments, shares, followers) — read for the operator's own analytics.

The App does not collect, store or process any data about viewers, commenters or other platform users, and it does not use cookies or tracking on this website.

3. How data is used

Solely to (a) upload and schedule the operator's own videos, (b) read the operator's own post statistics. Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and credentials are stored only on the operator's local machine, protected by operating-system access controls, and can be revoked at any time from Google, TikTok or Meta account settings. No server or database operated by the App stores personal data.

5. Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until revoked or expired. Local logs of published posts (post ID, time, status) are kept for the operator's records and can be deleted on request to the contact above. To revoke access: Google — myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok — Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Instagram/Facebook — Settings → Apps and Websites.

6. Third-party services

The App uses the YouTube API Services (subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy), TikTok for Developers APIs, and Meta Platform APIs, each under their own terms.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above reflects the latest version.
